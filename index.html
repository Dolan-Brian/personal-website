MyBlock

How I work

  • Decide what good looks like first

    Before I test something, I write down what a good result is. It keeps me honest about whether a change actually helped.

  • Say what the data can't tell you

    I'd rather a tool say "I don't know" than guess. Missing data is not a finding, and an answer should say how fresh its sources are.

  • Build for someone specific

    Most of what I build starts with a person I know and a question they keep asking. Seeing them use it is the real test.
